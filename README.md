# My Favorite Series: Black Clover

## About the Series
**Black Clover** is a *shonen* fantasy anime and manga by Yūki Tabata. It follows **Asta**, a boy born without magic in a world where almost everyone has it, who dreams of becoming the **Wizard King**.

## Why I Like It
- The story is about **never giving up**
- The fights and magic system are exciting
- The characters grow a lot over time

### Favorite Characters
1. Asta
2. Yuno
3. Noelle Silva
4. Yami Sukehiro
